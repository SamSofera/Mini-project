our story

Where stories steep

The Inkwell began with one rainy night, one stubborn deadline, and nowhere warm to write.

A few students decided campus needed a place that stayed open late, kept the lights low, and treated a laptop and a library book like the best of friends. So they did in a whim.

Today the Inkwell is still that same quiet hideout: strong coffee, soft music, and a window that always seems to be raining just enough.

What we hold dear

Slow is welcome

Order one cup and stay for hours. Nobody here is counting, and refills are always friendly.

Share the story

Our shelf runs on trust. Borrow a book, return it when you can, or leave one for the next reader.

Gentle on the planet

Ceramic mugs for dine-in, a discount for tumblers, and grounds composted for the campus garden.

Find your seat

We are on the ground floor of the SV Building, behind the courtyard. Follow the amber glow of the lamps and you will find us.
